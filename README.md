<summary>About Ruby 🌍</summary>

Ruby has an academic background in International Economics and Trade and extensive hands-on experience in B2B, B2C, and DTC cross-border business. Her journey of global exploration began there: from entering overseas markets to content distribution and user growth, she enjoys taking part in the entire process of bringing a project from idea to reality, with experience coordinating planning, execution, and review. She has led go-to-market (GTM) efforts that brought projects into overseas markets and acquired over 150,000 overseas users in total; these projects later went on to secure over RMB 100 million in funding.

She also loves solo travel. She has traveled alone across several countries, immersed herself in everyday local life, explored their cultures and customs, and formed treasured friendships with people she met along the way. Her understanding of the world comes both from markets and business and from the tangible, vivid connections between people.

Since going all in on AI in 2023, Ruby has been creating SaaS and AI Agent products and has led 5+ Agent projects to date. As an early co-builder of the AdventureX hackathon, she also works alongside creators and indie developers to bring ideas into the real world.

Starting in international business and cross-border work led to global markets, content, user growth, and AI products. Along the way, one motivation became clearer: turning an unclear idea into something real that people want to use, trust, and keep building with.

Working at the intersection of different worlds comes naturally: users and products, technology and markets, intuition and data, individual expression and collective work. The work often begins with a few questions: what are people truly trying to accomplish, what is holding them back, and how can the next step become clearer, more engaging, and easier to begin?

These experiences have brought several perspectives: the realities of cross-border business, the language of content and social distribution, the full path from acquisition to retention, and the way AI is changing products, workflows, and collaboration. The role of a connector feels especially fitting: translating insight into narrative, narrative into action, and action into outcomes that can be tested in the real world.

That means researching, writing, building workflows, reading data, talking with users, and working with engineering, design, and business partners to bring ideas into the real world. Execution is itself a way to understand a problem and test an idea.

A guiding belief is that the most valuable capability in the AI era is understanding faster, thinking more deeply, and turning ideas into grounded action. AI can amplify speed; clear judgment, genuine empathy, and sustained effort determine the direction.

The aim is to keep creating things that make people more capable, make work more meaningful, and help people across the world understand one another, while giving good ideas a chance to cross languages, borders, and differences in resources.

The exploration continues—

Learning about the world while using technology and creativity to build more connections and open up more possibilities.
